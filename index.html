What I build

Four things. Described by what they do for you.

Websites

People can find you, and book you, at two in the morning.

A site that answers the questions you keep answering by phone, and turns a stranger into an enquiry while you're asleep.

Web apps

The spreadsheet becomes something your team can actually use.

Bookings, jobs, stock, students, invoices — whatever you're tracking, shaped around how you already work instead of the other way around.

Automations

The copy-and-paste part of your day stops happening.

Information moves between your tools by itself. No more retyping the same order into three places and hoping you got it right.

AI assistants

The routine questions get answered without you.

An assistant that knows your business, handles the twenty easy messages, and hands you the two that need a human.
